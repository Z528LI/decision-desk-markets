口径与局限Definitions and limits
  • 组合里的低/中/高是当日全市场的三等分；单一特征表用的是五等分（最低/最高20%）。两者口径不同，所以“小市值”在两处范围不一样。Low / mid / high in the combinations are thirds of the market on the day; the single-trait table uses fifths (lowest / highest 20%). The two differ, so "small cap" covers a different range in each.
  • 可比股票＝当日与前一交易日都有成交、此前至少有41个交易日数据、涨跌幅在±35%以内；涨跌幅并列时成交额大的在前。Comparable stocks traded on the day and the session before, have at least 41 earlier sessions of data and moved less than ±35%; ties are broken by traded amount, larger first.
  • 过去每天的市值、市净率、股价＝今天的数值按复权价格比例回推，期间的增发回购未计入。Past market cap, price-to-book and price are today's values scaled back by the adjusted price ratio; share issuance and buybacks inside the window are not reflected.
  • 盈利用的是年报（数据文件 2026-11-23），之后的季报没有；窗口里较早的交易日用到了当时尚未披露的年报，所以这是事后描述，不是回测。Earnings are annual-report figures (data file 2026-11-23) with no later quarters; early sessions in the window use annual reports not yet published at the time, so this is description after the fact, not a backtest.
  • “题材炒作”只是近似：本地没有人气榜的历史，这里用高换手加业绩差来代替。"Speculative" is a proxy: there is no local history of popularity rankings, so high turnover plus weak earnings stands in for it.
  • 成交额与换手类风格用此前20日均值，避免把“今天大涨所以放量”算成风格。Liquidity and turnover styles use the previous 20-session average, so that "it jumped today, therefore it traded heavily" is not counted as a style.
  • 这一页说的是已经发生的事，不预测风格会持续多久，也不是买卖信号。This page describes what has happened; it does not predict how long a style will last and is not a trading signal.
